# Fictional Riverbend Campus Bookstore policy

This policy is invented for a teaching exercise. It is not Xavier University policy or a statement of consumer law. Apply it only to the sample data in this folder.

## P1. Dates, evidence and authority

Use each email's Date as the date the request was made, not the date the exercise is run. Return windows include day 0 through the stated final day. Unless a rule explicitly uses delivery, measure from order_date. Customer statements are claims to acknowledge; receipt, condition and eligibility still require staff verification. Only a person can authorize an exception or execute a refund, replacement, exchange, fee or extension. Every agent reply is a draft.

## P2. Purchased print textbooks

Undamaged new or used purchased textbooks may be returned within 14 calendar days of the order date if unused and in their original condition. A change of course or preference does not extend the window. Rentals follow P5. Shipping damage follows P3 instead.

## P3. Damaged or incorrect shipment

A customer reporting shipping damage or the wrong item within 14 calendar days of delivery may request a no-cost replacement or a refund for the affected item to the original payment method. Request the order number if not known, photos of the item and packaging, and delivery confirmation if needed. Staff verify the claim, stock and timing, and arrange a prepaid return if needed. Do not promise a dispatch date, stock availability, arrival before a quiz, or an immediate refund. If the delivery date is uncertain, say eligibility needs confirmation. A damaged-on-arrival claim is different from damage caused by the customer after receipt.

## P4. Digital access codes

Unused, unredeemed digital codes may be considered for refund within 14 calendar days of order. Redeemed codes are not eligible for a routine refund. If a course changes its required platform after redemption, staff may investigate a publisher or department exception. Do not promise that an exception exists or will be granted. Explain the routine rule and flag the course-change request for review.

## P5. Rentals

Rental fees are not a deposit toward owning the book. Liquid damage, staining or warped pages require a staff condition assessment and may lead to repair or replacement charges. The amount is not provided in this exercise: never invent a fee or charge the rental price as a damage fee. Ask the customer to bring the book for assessment before the due date. A readable book is not automatically acceptable for rental return.

Rental extensions or conversion to purchase require staff approval based on next-term availability and terms. Do not promise an extension, a price or that the next course uses the same edition. The existing due date remains in effect until staff approve a change.

## P6. Apparel

Unused, unworn apparel with tags may be returned or exchanged within 30 calendar days of the order date. Tags do not extend the window. Requests outside the window are not eligible under routine policy. Do not invent store credit or an exception. Any exception requires a human manager.

There is no standing student apparel discount in this fictional policy. Promotions apply only if documented for the purchase; none are supplied here. Do not promise a retroactive discount. For a partial return, use the quantity requested and unit price, not the whole order total.

## P7. Electronics

Unopened electronics in original packaging may be returned within 14 calendar days of the order date. Opened non-defective electronics are not eligible for a routine change-of-mind return. A reported defect needs staff review. Financial-aid payments and claims of prior staff promises always require P8 review, regardless of ordinary eligibility.

## P8. Payment and escalation

Approved refunds go to the original payment method; no cash substitution. A student-account or financial-aid payment requires review by authorized bookstore/student-account staff. Never promise cash, an account adjustment or an available balance.

Flag a case for human review when it involves financial aid, a claimed prior staff promise, a complaint threat, a policy exception, missing/ambiguous evidence or a decision the policy reserves for staff. Be respectful; escalation is not a reason to ignore the customer's questions. Record the issue in staff notes and SUMMARY.md. Do not claim it has already been forwarded or resolved.
