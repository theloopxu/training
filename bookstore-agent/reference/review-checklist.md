# Review every draft

- Does the sender match the order record, item and requested quantity?
- Is the request date taken from the email rather than today's date?
- Does every question receive an answer or a clearly stated need for human review?
- Does the proposal follow the cited policy, without inventing an exception, charge, discount, stock level or deadline?
- Are customer claims distinguished from confirmed records?
- Are payment restrictions and human approval boundaries preserved?
- Does the reply avoid claiming that anything has been sent, refunded, shipped, reserved or escalated already?
- Does the file clearly identify itself as a draft, with internal evidence separate from the customer-facing text?

A self-check supports review. It does not replace a person's approval.
