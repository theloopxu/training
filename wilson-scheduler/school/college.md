## The Management department in one paragraph

Management offers 40 credit-bearing sections this fall. The two core courses dominate: Organizational Management (MGMT 200, 12 sections) and Business and Professional Communication (MGMT 201, 8 sections). ENTR 305 and MGMT 305 are cross-listed and always meet together. Co-op and internship sections are arranged individually and need no room or time. Faculty load capacity is 51 sections. Most of the slack sits with instructors qualified for only one or two courses, so who-teaches-what is the real constraint.
