# Wilson Management faculty survey, Fall 2027

`responses-fall-2027.xlsx` is the department's preference form export, one row per full-time instructor. `responses-fall-2027.csv` holds the same rows.

The form asks six questions: name, ranked Fall 2027 courses, times the instructor can't teach in Fall 2027, the same two questions for Spring 2028, and free-text notes for the chair.

Adjuncts do not answer the form. They are available for one-night evening blocks only, at the load listed in `../school/management-faculty.csv`.

Answers are typed by people. Course codes appear in several formats, and the free text carries real constraints. Every rule taken from the free text must quote the answer it came from.

Regenerate: `python -X utf8 Loop/scripts/wilson_survey.py`.
